0% ready

This rocket takes you back to orbit, on your way home. Finish all three jobs, then press IGNITE.

1. Rocket fuel: 0%

The fuel factory splits Martian ice into hydrogen and oxygen, then mixes the hydrogen with carbon dioxide from the Mars air to make methane. Methane + oxygen = rocket fuel. It needs ice and uses energy.

2. Solar storms survived: 0/3

A solar storm starts when the Sun throws out a huge cloud of fast particles (scientists call it a CME). Stay alive until 3 storms pass: hide in the base or turn on the shield [R].

Send your Mars discoveries to Earth from the command console inside the base: stand next to it and press [G]. The radio uses energy.
