Where the data comes from

Every number that matters in MineMars comes from open NASA data. The data is saved inside the game, it is not live.

Solar stormsReal CMEs (clouds of particles thrown by the Sun) listed by NASA DONKI. Real data from .
RadiationCuriosity rover RAD instrument at Gale Crater, from NASA RadLab (OSDR): 6.93 uGy per hour, measured 16/11 to 10/12/2022.
Mars factsGravity 38% of Earth, a day of 24 h 39 min and 43% of Earth sunlight, from the NASA Mars Fact Sheet.
WeatherTemperature and air pressure from the NASA InSight lander, sols 675 to 681 (October 2020).
Crew needsOxygen, water and food per person from the NASA Baseline Values and Assumptions Document. Water recycling of 98% like the Space Station.
Making airMOXIE on the Perseverance rover made 122 g of oxygen on Mars (NASA, 2023).
HealthCareer radiation limit of 600 mSv (NASA-STD-3001). Bones lose about 1% of density a month in space without exercise (NASA, 2023).

Game speed, battery size and storm length are scaled so a mission fits in a few minutes. Art, textures and sounds are drawn by code, with no NASA logo. Parts of the code were written with AI assistance.

The fuel factory splits Martian ice into hydrogen and oxygen, then mixes the hydrogen with carbon dioxide from the Mars air to make methane. Methane + oxygen = rocket fuel. It needs ice and uses energy.

        

        

          
          
          
        

      


      

        
2. Solar storms survived: 0/3

        
A solar storm starts when the Sun throws out a huge cloud of fast particles (scientists call it a CME). Stay alive until 3 storms pass: hide in the base or turn on the shield [R].

Send your Mars discoveries to Earth from the command console inside the base: stand next to it and press [G]. The radio uses energy.
